## Delete Your Taqwa Habit Account

Last updated: September 2026

You can delete your Taqwa Habit account directly from the app. Account deletion is permanent and will remove your associated data. Please follow the steps below.

### How to Delete Your Account

- Open the Taqwa Habit app.

- Go to Profile.

- Open Settings.

- Scroll to the bottom of the Settings page.

- Tap Delete Account.

- Follow the confirmation steps to permanently delete your account and associated data.

Important

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Once deleted, your account data cannot be recovered.

If you are unable to access the account deletion option in the app, please contact us through the support method provided on the Uxhan Forge website.
